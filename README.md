# [LA High Holidays](https://wuisabel-gif.github.io/high_holidays_5787/) 

A simple directory for finding **Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur services, meals, and community events across Los Angeles**.

The goal of LA High Holidays is to make it easier for students, young adults, newcomers, and anyone in the LA Jewish community to answer a simple question:

> **Where can I go for the High Holidays?**

Instead of searching through individual synagogue, Hillel, and community websites, this site brings different options together in one place.

## Features

- 🔎 Search High Holiday events and communities
- 📍 Browse options across Los Angeles neighborhoods
- 🎓 Find student-friendly options, including Hillel and Chabad
- 🍽️ Find services that include meals or community gatherings
- 💻 Find livestream and online options
- 💰 Filter for free and lower-cost events
- 🕍 Explore different traditions and service styles
- 📅 Quickly see events happening today
- 🔗 Go directly to official registration or event pages

## Communities

The directory includes organizations such as:

- USC Hillel
- Chabad at USC
- UCLA Hillel
- IKAR
- Nefesh
- Temple Israel of Hollywood
- Bayit Shelanu

More synagogues, Hillels, Chabad houses, and independent Jewish communities across Los Angeles can be added over time.

## Why I Made This

Los Angeles has a huge and diverse Jewish community, but High Holiday information is spread across many different websites.

This can be especially difficult for:

- college students
- people who recently moved to Los Angeles
- young adults looking for a community
- people who are not members of a synagogue
- visitors spending the holidays in LA
- anyone trying to compare service styles, locations, prices, and registration requirements

LA High Holidays is an attempt to make that information easier to discover.
